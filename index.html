The Rubellite Method

The Legacy Architecture™

Structure. Shelter. Settle. Every Rubellite engagement moves through three deliberate stages: Structure the corporation and holdings so wealth can be organized efficiently; Shelter the estate from unnecessary tax, probate friction, and creditor exposure; Settle the succession so control, value, and intent transfer on your terms.

Questions?

Frequently asked questions

What makes Rubellite different from other wealth advisors? +

We specialize exclusively in corporate estate and succession planning. Rather than general investment advice, we coordinate across tax, legal, and investment strategy for incorporated business owners. This specialized focus means deeper expertise in your specific situation.

Are you just another insurance broker? +

Not at all. We view insurance not as a standalone product, but as a strategic lever within a complete financial plan. As wealth advisors, our core focus is helping you structure, protect, and grow your overall wealth — integrating tax planning, corporate strategies, and estate design to ensure every strategy actively protects and advances your long-term goals.

What if I already have a financial plan or structure in place? +

That is no problem at all — in fact, we would be happy to audit your current setup at no cost to you. Wealth isn't stagnant; it is constantly growing and evolving, and your planning and corporate structure should adapt right alongside it. We can provide a second set of eyes to ensure you aren't leaving any opportunities on the table.

What happens to my business if I don't have a succession plan? +

The transfer will happen with or without one. Without documented direction, control and value are divided by default rules written for no one in particular — assessed, probated, and taxed before a single wish is honoured. A plan is the only voice you keep in the room. Our work is to make certain it speaks clearly, divides fairly, and costs the people in it as little as the law allows.

Is there a minimum to work with Rubellite? +

No published minimum. We qualify by complexity and fit rather than an asset figure: an incorporated business, significant real property, or an estate that spans generations usually signals that coordinated planning will repay its cost many times over. If we are not the right firm for your situation, we will say so in the first conversation — and point you toward someone who is.
